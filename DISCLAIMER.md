# Responsible Use and Disclosure

This repository is for educational documentation and defensive security learning.

Only assess systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Follow the agreed scope, rate limits, time window, and stop conditions. Do not perform denial-of-service testing against public systems. Do not publish third-party IP addresses, domains, raw scan outputs, or identifying screenshots without the owner's permission.

The observations in this repository are preliminary and limited by the supplied evidence. They are not a certification, guarantee of security, or proof of exploitability.
